OpenLKA DATA USE NOTICE

The OpenLKA dataset is subject to the data terms published by its maintainers.
The MIT license for OpenLKA code and DBC files does not automatically apply
to the dataset data.

Conditions for shared derived tables:
- Cite the OpenLKA IEEE ITSC 2025 paper.
- Attribute OpenLKA in derived work.
- Do not attempt to re-identify drivers, vehicles, or people.
- Do not redistribute raw video.
- Share derived tables only with these terms attached.

Official terms and citation:
https://openlka.github.io/

This notice summarizes the published terms; it does not grant additional
permissions or replace the dataset maintainers' terms.
